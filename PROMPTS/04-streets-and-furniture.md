# 04: Streets and furniture

Reference code: `code/oldtown/streets.js`, `code/oldtown/furniture.js`,
`code/oldtown/streetprops.js`, `code/oldtown/bridges.js`, `code/oldtown/river.js`.
Positions of every lamp, seat, parked vehicle and movable prop: `data/oldtown-plan.json`
(`furniture`).

Prompt:

> Lay the ground and the street furniture of the old town from its plan:
>
> - Surfaces, never overlapping: cobbles (11 cm stones) on lanes with a 0.5 m sett gutter in the
>   middle; granite setts (14 x 20 cm) on squares in panels between flagstone bands; flagstone
>   pavements (50 x 80 cm) behind granite kerbs (0.14 m high), rounded at junction corners
>   (radius 2 m) with build-outs 9 m either side of each junction; asphalt carriageways with zebra
>   crossings (3 m wide, 0.6 m from the crossing carriageway); setts on the tram avenue and in the
>   parking bays; tram rails; gravel courtyards; a gravel tree strip on the promenade.
> - River: embankment walls from the bed to the street with a darker water-stained band, a string
>   course at quay height and granite coping; paved lower quays with mooring bollards and an iron
>   rail at the water; stairs down from the street; parapets along the top.
> - Bridges: three stone arches on two piers with pointed cutwaters, barrel vaults with ring
>   stones, balustraded parapets with candelabra over the piers; an iron bowstring footbridge.
> - Lanterns: cast-iron posts at the kerb every ~20 m alternating sides; wall lanterns on arms in
>   the lanes every ~13 m alternating sides; posts round the squares (one per ~18 m of edge);
>   posts along the promenade parapet every 16 m; candelabra on the bridges. A soft additive light
>   pool on the ground under each.
> - Trees on the avenue (two rows), the promenade (lindens every 8 m), the cathedral square (rows
>   every 10 m), the small squares.
> - Market fountain (radius 3.6 m) and stalls; a well; cafe terraces (round tables with two chairs
>   and parasols) in front of every cafe or restaurant on a square or the promenade; benches with a
>   bin beside each; bike racks, scooters, bins along pavements; bollards across lane mouths;
>   finger posts; tram stops and overhead wires (poles at both kerbs every 30 m, contact wire at
>   5.9 m); traffic signals on every junction corner (4.6 m poles, pedestrian and vehicle heads).
> - Parked cars in about 70% of the bays, 0.9 to 2.4 m apart.
> - Every item claims a keep-out circle so nothing is placed on top of anything else, and keeps
>   clear of the pedestrian walking lines.
