# 03: Landmarks

Reference code: `code/oldtown/buildings/landmarks.js` (buildCathedral, buildMarketHall,
buildTownHall, buildTower), `code/oldtown/river.js` (buildWall), `code/oldtown/prison.js`.

Prompt:

> Build these landmarks for a central European old town (metres, y up, ground at y = 4):
>
> 1. Gothic cathedral on a 32 x 46 m site facing the square: warm limestone (#f4e8d6); nave about
>    14.7 m wide and 20 m to the eaves with aisles 9.2 m high, flying buttresses with pinnacles, a
>    polygonal apse; a west front of two square towers (about 8.4 m) rising 35 m in four stages
>    with lancets and a belfry, corner pinnacles and octagonal copper spires about 26 m tall with
>    lucarnes; between the towers a portal of four stepped pointed archivolts with colonnettes, a
>    trumeau and tympanum, a gable over it, a gallery of blind arches with statues, and a rose
>    window; stained glass that glows at dusk.
> 2. Market hall on a 20 x 26 m site inside the market square: brick end walls each with an open
>    round arch (up to 6.5 m wide, stone dressings) and a lunette; green cast-iron columns
>    (#2c3a34) in two lines at 27% and 73% of the width; nave 11.5 m high under a 30 degree glazed
>    roof, lean-to aisles 6.8 m; two rows of stalls with striped canopies inside, hanging lamps.
> 3. Town hall on a 24 x 26 m site closing the market's north side: sandstone (#faf2e4), a
>    rusticated arcade ground floor (4.8 m), storeys of 5.2, 4.4 and 3.6 m, a central balcony,
>    quoins, a grand cornice, a copper hip roof at 32 degrees; a clock tower (up to 7.5 m square)
>    rising from the middle of the front 16 m above the cornice: gold clock faces, an open belfry
>    with louvres, an octagonal drum with oculi, a copper dome, lantern, spire and gold ball.
> 4. A bell tower on the small cafe square: about 5.6 m square and 24 m high with three string
>    courses and the same clock top.
> 5. The town wall: crenellated, gate towers where the six streets meet it, water gates where the
>    river passes.
> 6. Optional: an island jail in the river (50 x 20 m island) with a cell block, gatehouse,
>    service wing, a walled yard and a watchtower with a searchlight, and a stone footbridge.
